Bubble Shooter Tips: Aiming, Bank Shots and Clearing Groups

Improve bubble shooter decisions with color matching, aiming lanes, wall rebounds and planning the next shot.

1. Read the color groups

Find connected bubbles of the same color. A shot is useful when it joins a matching group of at least three or removes a connection supporting other bubbles.

2. Plan the angle

Trace the path before firing. If the direct route is blocked, look for an open lane or a wall rebound when the game supports it.

3. Think about what falls

Removing a connected group may cause bubbles attached only to it to fall too. Targeting a supporting group can clear more than the bubbles hit directly.

4. Manage the next bubble

If the next color is shown, use that information. Avoid leaving a large isolated group that will be difficult to reach with the upcoming color.
