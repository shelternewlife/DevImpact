Block Puzzle Tips: Clear Rows and Keep the Board Open

Practical block puzzle tips for placing shapes, clearing rows and columns, and preserving space for future pieces.

1. Inspect all pieces

Consider the full set before placing the first shape. A convenient move may block the only space needed for a larger piece.

2. Keep open areas

Avoid filling the board into narrow holes that accept only one exact shape. Broad open spaces give you more options when new pieces arrive.

3. Clear lines with purpose

A complete row or column creates room, but do not damage a useful layout just to clear one line. Prefer placements that also leave a flexible board.

4. Watch isolated gaps

Small empty spaces can become unusable when surrounding cells are filled. Check whether a placement creates a pocket future shapes cannot reach.
