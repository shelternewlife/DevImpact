Liquid Sort Puzzle: Rules, Planning and Avoiding Dead Ends

Understand liquid sort puzzle rules and plan pours by matching top colors, checking tube capacity and preserving empty space.

1. Know when a pour is valid

In common rules, pour only when the destination is empty or its top color matches, and enough capacity remains. The top layer moves first.

2. Keep an empty tube available

An empty tube is working space. It lets you move a top layer temporarily to reveal a hidden color. Avoid filling every empty tube too early.

3. Build one color at a time

Consolidate matching colors without trapping another color underneath. Before pouring, consider what becomes exposed in the source tube.

4. Avoid aimless moves

Ask what a pour unlocks: does it reveal a needed color, complete a tube or create space? Repeating the same transfer can create a dead end.
