Job Connect vs Job Search: Comparing Career Discovery Apps

Both relate to finding work, but their listed purposes differ. Compare the published descriptions and official listings for current details.

Job Connect

Explore job opportunities, save listings, set job alerts and discover job roles and career paths.

View Job Connect

Job Search - Career - find job

Browse job categories and listings, with matching based on skills, qualifications and preferences. Its listing also refers to notifications for new jobs.

View Job Search

How to choose

Review current store screenshots and descriptions. Job Connect explicitly describes saving jobs and setting alerts; Job Search describes browsing categories and matching listings to a candidate profile. Neither listing guarantees employment.
