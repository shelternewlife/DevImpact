ZyCrush vs Palace Match: Comparing Two Match-3 Games

Both use match-3 mechanics, but their published descriptions highlight different themes and progression details.

ZyCrush

Candy-themed match-3 with levels, objectives and limited moves. Its description includes combos, coins and optional boosters or shop elements.

View ZyCrush

Palace Match

Fruit-themed match-3 on an 8×8 board, with limited-move levels and endless mode. It tracks score, best score, moves, coins and level.

View Palace Match

How they differ

Explore ZyCrush for its candy theme and objective-based levels. Explore Palace Match if an 8×8 fruit board, score tracking and endless play are more relevant. Check each Google Play listing for current details.
