Palace Match

Palace Match is a fruit match-3 puzzle game built around quick, satisfying board play. Swap adjacent fruits on an 8×8 board, match three or more, trigger cascades, earn coins and gain XP as you progress through palace levels.

Get it on Google Play ↗View app screenshots

About Palace Match

The board uses rows and columns of colorful fruit. Swapping nearby pieces creates matches of three or more, clearing them and allowing the board to refill and form new cascades. Score goals, move limits and combos add a puzzle challenge while keeping the controls simple. The Google Play listing names apples, bananas, oranges, grapes, watermelon, lemon, cherry, kiwi, mango, pineapple, dragonfruit and lychee among the fruit pieces. Combo chains boost scores and earn coins; matches can add XP and help unlock more fruit variety. Choose limited-move rounds for a focused challenge or endless mode for a more relaxed session. The store description also mentions score, best score, moves, coins, level progress and comfort settings for sound, music and haptics.
