Bubble Fury: Bubble Shooter

Bubble Fury is a fast arcade bubble shooter. Aim carefully, shoot bubbles and match groups of the same color before the wall reaches the bottom of the screen. Each shot is an opportunity to clear space, build a combo and improve your high score.

About Bubble Fury: Bubble Shooter

Unlike bubble puzzle games built around long level maps, Bubble Fury is described on Google Play as an arcade-style challenge where progress comes from improving aim, score and survival. Drag to aim and release to shoot. Match three or more bubbles of the same color to pop them and clear space. Precision shots and wall rebounds can help reach difficult angles. Successful matches build combo chains, while surviving longer brings tougher conditions and additional bubble types. Coins are earned through gameplay, and the experience is designed for quick sessions as well as longer high-score attempts.

How to play

  1. Drag to aim the launcher.
  2. Release to shoot a bubble.
  3. Match at least three bubbles of the same color to clear them.
  4. Use wall rebounds to reach awkward angles and build combos.
