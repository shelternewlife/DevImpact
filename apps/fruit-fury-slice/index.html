Fruit Fury Slice

Fruit Fury Slice is a fast-paced fruit-slicing arcade game where every swipe matters. Slice flying fruit, react to sudden danger, build combo chains and work toward higher scores in short, skill-focused sessions.

About Fruit Fury Slice

The core loop is built around precision and timing. Swipe across fruit as it flies across the screen, use responsive touch controls and create combo chains to improve your score. Avoid bombs, because careless swipes can end a promising run. As you move through levels, object speed, spawn patterns and challenge intensity gradually increase. The Google Play listing describes coin and gem rewards, offline play support, clear objectives and a gradual difficulty curve. Early stages introduce the mechanics, while later levels call for faster reactions and more accurate swipes.
