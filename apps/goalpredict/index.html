GoalPredict: Match Predictions

GoalPredict is a data-driven football prediction and tactical analytics app. It presents structured match forecasts and team insights to help users examine potential outcomes, expected goals and team strengths before kickoff.

Get it on Google Play ↗View app screenshots

About GoalPredict: Match Predictions

The Google Play listing says GoalPredict calculates win, draw and loss probabilities alongside Expected Goals (xG), using ELO power ratings and a Bivariate Poisson model. Match DNA visualizes nine tactical dimensions, including attacking potency, defensive resilience, high pressure, tempo and discipline. An Evidence Matrix and Data Trust Index provide context about the quality and completeness of the match data used. The listing describes coverage across more than 115 global leagues and competitions, including major European leagues and selected competitions in South America, Asia and Africa. Forecasts are statistical estimates and must not be interpreted as guarantees of match results.

Frequently asked questions

What does GoalPredict do?

It provides football match forecasts and tactical analytics based on statistical modelling.

Which prediction metrics are included?

The listing mentions win/draw/loss probabilities, Expected Goals (xG), ELO ratings and a Bivariate Poisson model.

What is Match DNA?

It is a comparison view across nine tactical dimensions, including attacking potency, defensive resilience, pressure, tempo and discipline.

What is the Data Trust Index?

It gives context about the quality and completeness of the match input data; it is not a guarantee of prediction accuracy.

How many competitions are covered?

The Google Play description states coverage across 115+ global leagues and competitions for available fixtures.

Are predictions guaranteed outcomes?

No. The store listing explicitly says the Data Trust Index and forecasts should not be interpreted as guarantees of match results.
