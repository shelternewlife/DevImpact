Célibataire: Rencontres & Chat

Célibataire: Rencontres & Chat (listed in the app experience as Single: Meet & Chat) helps people discover nearby connections based on what they are looking for. The Google Play listing describes three intentions in one app: dating, friendship and professional networking.

About Célibataire: Rencontres & Chat

Create a profile with photos, a biography, prompts and interests. Browse profiles and use filters for age, distance and intentions. The listing also describes profile likes and SuperSwipe, a women-first first-message approach, and matches that expire after 24 hours with options to extend or send another match. For friendship, users can look for people to go out with, travel with or chat with without romantic pressure. For professional networking, the app brings people with shared ambitions into the same interface while keeping intentions distinct. Conversations support text messages, photos and voice notes. Local storage helps users continue exploring when the connection is unstable.

This page is based on the app's Google Play listing. App features and availability may change; consult the official store page for the latest information.
