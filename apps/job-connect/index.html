Job Connect

Job Connect is a job-search app designed to help users explore employment opportunities, discover roles and career paths, and keep track of interesting listings. The Google Play listing specifically describes job search, saved jobs and job alerts.

Get it on Google Play ↗View app screenshots

About Job Connect

Use Job Connect to look for job opportunities that align with your skills, interests and career goals. The store description also highlights learning more about different job roles, exploring career paths and considering opportunities that may better fit your lifestyle. Save listings to revisit them and set job alerts to follow new opportunities. The app supports the job discovery process; actual vacancies, application requirements and hiring decisions are determined by employers and the listings available.

This page is based on the app's Google Play listing. App features and availability may change; consult the official store page for the latest information.

How to use Job Connect

  1. Explore available job opportunities and browse roles or career paths.
  2. Open a listing to review its details.
  3. Save jobs that you want to revisit.
  4. Set job alerts to hear about relevant new opportunities when available.

Frequently asked questions

Yes. The Google Play listing describes searching job opportunities and job listings.

Can I save jobs?

Yes. Saving job listings is one of the features stated in the store description.

Does Job Connect support alerts?

Yes. The listing says users can set up job alerts.

Can I explore career paths?

Yes. The app description mentions exploring job roles and career paths.
