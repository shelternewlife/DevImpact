Privacy · Transparency

سياسة الخصوصية

This page explains the privacy scope of the DevImpact website and how to find the applicable privacy information for an individual app.

1. Scope

This notice applies to the public DevImpact website hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not replace or override privacy policies, disclosures or Data safety information that may apply to an individual تطبيق أندرويد.

2. Website interactions and external services

This website is a static portfolio and information site. It provides links to Google Play, GitHub and email. When you follow an external link or contact a third party, that service may process information under its own privacy terms. GitHub Pages hosting and delivery may involve technical request information handled by GitHub under its own terms.

The website does not provide account registration, user profiles, payment forms or an on-site messaging service.

3. App-specific privacy information

Each تطبيق أندرويد may have its own data practices and privacy disclosures. Before installing or using an app, review its Data safety section and the developer-provided سياسة الخصوصية link on its official Google Play listing, where available. Those app-specific disclosures apply to the relevant app.
