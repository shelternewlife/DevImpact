What defines arcade gameplay?

Arcade games often emphasize short sessions, direct controls, timing, aiming or quick reactions. Fruit Fury Slice asks you to swipe through flying fruit and avoid bombs. Bubble Fury asks you to aim and connect matching bubbles. Both are arcade-oriented, but use different mechanics.
