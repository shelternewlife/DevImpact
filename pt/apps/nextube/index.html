Nextube

Nextube is a neon-style liquid sorting puzzle. Tap one tube and then another to pour liquid, organize colors and work toward a clean board where each tube contains one color.

Get it on Google Play ↗View app screenshots

Sobre Nextube

The rules are simple to learn: select a tube, then select a destination tube. Liquid can be moved only when the top color matches and the destination has space. The challenge comes from planning the order of moves as more layers and stages appear. The Google Play listing describes Undo and Reset controls for reconsidering a sequence, along with progressive levels, stars, coins and profile progression. It also mentions cosmetic tube skins and backgrounds, short sessions and an offline-friendly puzzle experience. The game is designed around tap, pour, assess and plan.

This page is based on the app's Google Play listing. App features and availability may change; consult the official store page for the latest information.

How to play Nextube

  1. Tap a tube to select the top layer of liquid.
  2. Tap a destination tube to pour; the top colors must match and the destination must have enough space.
  3. Continue sorting until each tube contains a single color.
  4. Use Undo or Reset when you need to rethink a move.

Frequently asked questions

What is the goal in Nextube?

The goal described on Google Play is to finish with one color in each tube.

How do I pour liquid?

Tap one tube and then another. A pour is allowed when the top color matches and the destination has space.

Can I undo a move?

Yes. The listing describes Undo and Reset controls.

Does the puzzle get harder?

Yes. The description says new layers and more complex planning appear as progression continues.

Is Nextube suitable for short sessions?

The listing describes short sessions and an offline-friendly puzzle loop.
