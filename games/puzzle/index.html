Explore Android puzzle formats including match-3, bubble shooter, block puzzles and liquid sorting, with links to DevImpact games.

Understand the puzzle formats

Match-3: connect three or more matching pieces and plan around objectives or move limits.

Bubble shooter: aim colored bubbles at matching groups and consider angles and rebounds.

Block puzzle: position shapes to complete rows while preserving open space.

Liquid sort: transfer top color layers between tubes while respecting matching and capacity rules.
